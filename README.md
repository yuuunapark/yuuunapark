# Hi, I'm Yuna Park 👋

I'm a graduate student at **Yonsei University** (M.S., Electrical & Electronic Engineering), researching **LLM Safety & Alignment**. I'm also a student researcher at **KIST** (Korea Institute of Science and Technology).

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **LLM Safety & Alignment** — jailbreak susceptibility, prompt-based behavior induction, red teaming
- **LLM Reasoning & Hallucination** — robustness and reliability of large language models
